---

Joseph D. Grant County Park is located toward the end end of the Bay Area in San Jose, about 40-minute drive from Palo Alto. We came here in early spring trying to catch some wild flowers. Didn't see much blossom yet but the scenery on the hill was quite a treat.

<!--truncate-->

There were not too many people in the park. Nearby the park there are numerous biking routes. Right off the parking lot there is a small lake.

We shall come back later for more flowers.
