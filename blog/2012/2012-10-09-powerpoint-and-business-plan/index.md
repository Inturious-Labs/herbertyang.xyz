In today's GSBGEN239 - Executive Communication class, Professor Schramm talked about some basic rules of doing powerpoint, something I've spent a fair amount of time doing in my past life. In January 2011, I gave this talk to the graduate students of [China Central Academy of Fine Arts (CAFA/中央美院)](http://www.cafa.edu.cn), upon a request of a professor friend from CAFA. The business ideas in this ppt are very simple and should be familiar to all entrepreneurs / corporate warriors. What's special about this PPT is that it marked a water-shed point in the continuing evolution of my powerpoint style, which was going to be heavily influenced by Steve Jobs and well illustrated in the landmark book about powerpoint-making, "[Presentation Zen: Simple Ideas on Presentation Design and Delivery](http://amzn.com/0321525655)" by Garr Reynolds.
